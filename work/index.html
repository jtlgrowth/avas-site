We value your privacy

This page keeps two settings on your device: whether the music is on, and whether you asked for calmer motion. One thing comes from a third party, and only when you allow it: the music, which plays in YouTube's own player.

What that actually means
  • Kept on your device, never sent anywhere: your sound and motion choices, and this answer.
  • The booking form is our own, so it loads either way. It runs a Cloudflare check that stops spam bookings.
  • Played by YouTube when you accept or tap Sound: Brooklyn Duo's cover of Landslide in YouTube's player, kept out of sight, which may set its own cookies once it plays.
  • If you decline: nothing third party loads on its own. The music waits until you tap Sound.

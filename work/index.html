We value your privacy

This page keeps two settings on your device: whether the keyboard sounds are on, and whether you asked for calmer motion. The booking calendar is loaded from Calendly, a third party, and only when you allow it.

What that actually means
  • Kept on your device, never sent anywhere: your sound and motion choices, and this answer.
  • Loaded from Calendly when you accept: their booking widget, which sets its own cookies so it can remember your slot.
  • If you decline: nothing third party loads, and the booking panel gives you a plain link instead.

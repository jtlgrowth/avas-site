We value your privacy

This page keeps two settings on your device: whether the music is on, and whether you asked for calmer motion. Two things come from third parties, and only when you allow them: the booking calendar from Calendly, and the music, which plays in YouTube's own player.

What that actually means
  • Kept on your device, never sent anywhere: your sound and motion choices, and this answer.
  • Loaded from Calendly when you accept: their booking widget, which sets its own cookies so it can remember your slot.
  • Played by YouTube when you accept or tap Sound: Brooklyn Duo's cover of Landslide in YouTube's player, kept out of sight, which may set its own cookies once it plays.
  • If you decline: nothing third party loads on its own. The booking panel gives you a plain link, and the music waits until you tap Sound.

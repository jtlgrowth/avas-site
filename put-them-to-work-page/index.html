01The five jobs

You do not have a hundred problems. You have one of five. Pick the one that matches the thing you are tired of doing, then build only that today.

The Finder

Goes looking so you stop scrolling. Leads, suppliers, rates, PR spots, competitor prices.

The Sorter

Decides what is worth your time and throws out the rest. This is where most of the value hides.

The Writer

Produces the thing you rewrite every week. Captions, proposals, itineraries, product descriptions, replies.

The Answerer

Handles the same questions you answer forty times a month, in your words, without you.

The Watcher

Tells you when something changes. Stock, reviews, deadlines, follow ups that have gone cold.

03Your six prompts

Use them in order. The brackets are the only part you change. Prompt two is the one everybody wants to skip, and it is the one that decides whether any of this works.

Prompt 01
Pick the job
My business is [what you do, in one line].

Here is what I actually did at work last week:
1. [thing]
2. [thing]
3. [thing]

Which of these is a Finder, a Sorter, a Writer, an Answerer
or a Watcher job?

Pick the one I should build first and tell me why that one.
Be honest in the list. A task you wish you did is not a task.
Prompt 02
Write the rule
I want my [job type] to handle [task].

Before we build anything, interview me. One question at a time.

Keep going until you can write down what a good result looks
like, what a bad result looks like, what you must never do,
and what to do when you are unsure.

Then write the rule back to me in plain English.
Answer its questions properly. This is where all the value is.
Prompt 03
Give the job to your employee
You are my [name your AI employee].

Your job is [task].
Your rule is: [paste the rule from prompt 2].
My business: [describe it].

Here is real work to do right now:
[paste something real from your business]

Do it now. Then tell me which part of my rule was unclear.
Real work, never a made up example. A sample will lie to you.
Prompt 04
Fix it
That was wrong because [say exactly what was wrong].

Do not fix the output.

Tell me which part of my rule caused it, then rewrite that part.
You will use this one more than any other. That is normal.
Prompt 05
Now make it beautiful
Now make it look good.

Rules: one accent colour only. Two fonts maximum. One element
is clearly the biggest. Generous white space. No purple to blue
gradient. Do not centre everything by default. Use my real
words, never placeholder text.

My brand colours are [hex codes].
Make it work on my phone too.
If you do not know your hex codes, say your colours in words and ask it to pick them.
Prompt 06
Keep it forever
Save everything we agreed as a permanent instruction for this
employee, so I never have to explain it again.

Keep it under 400 words.
Do this before you close your laptop tonight, or you start from zero tomorrow.

04How to talk to it

You are not writing prompts. You are briefing someone. Nobody in that room saw me type a perfect paragraph, because I never do.

01
Give the context once.Not every single time. Tell it about your business properly, once, then stop repeating yourself.
02
Say what done looks like.Before it starts. Not after you already hate the output.
03
Let it interview you.If it is not asking you questions, it is guessing, and you will get a guess back.
04
Give it your real work.Never a sample, never a pretend client. Real work breaks the rule and shows you the gap.
05
When it is wrong, fix the rule.Not the output. A wrong answer almost always means a vague rule.
06
Say what you do not want.Not just what you want. This is the one everybody skips and it is the fastest fix there is.

Half sentences are fine. So are corrections, and so is Taglish. "ang oa naman nyan" is a completely valid instruction and I use it constantly.

You already know how to do this. You have onboarded a VA. You have briefed a designer. Same skill, and this one never gets tired of you repeating yourself.

05Make it beautiful

Most people stop the second the thing works, and then it looks like a school project and they are embarrassed to show anyone. Two more minutes fixes that.

  • One accent colour. Not three.
  • Two fonts maximum.
  • One thing is obviously the biggest thing on the screen.
  • White space is not wasted space.
  • No purple to blue gradient. That is the AI uniform and everyone can spot it.
  • Do not centre everything by default.
  • Your real words, never placeholder text. Placeholder text hides the fact that your copy does not fit.

06When you get stuck

  • It gave you something generic. Your rule was vague. Go back to prompt 2 and answer the questions properly.
  • It keeps forgetting. You never ran prompt 6. Run it.
  • It sounds like a robot. You never told it how you sound. Paste five things you wrote yourself and ask it to copy your voice.
  • It made something up. It guesses when it does not know. Tell it to ask instead of guessing when it is unsure, and put that in the rule.
  • You cannot decide what to build. Pick the thing you did last week that you resented most. That one.
  • It broke and you cannot see why. Paste the error straight back in and say what you were trying to do. That works far more often than it should.

The minimum win today. A written rule, one real output, and it does not look like a school project. That is done. Anything past that is a bonus.

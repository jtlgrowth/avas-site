TERMS, IN PLAIN SENTENCES

Where every number stops.

Scope creep is a definition problem. Here are the definitions, in the same words that appear in the contract.

We do not sell unlimited
We sell a number of requests in progress at a time, with an uncapped queue behind it. Same promise, written honestly.
Revisions
Two rounds per asset. A round is one consolidated set of comments, delivered together. Comments that arrive after the next round starts begin a third round, quoted before we start.
Turnaround
A reply inside one business day, Manila. Clocks run on Philippine business days, excluding Philippine public holidays.
If we are waiting on you
More than 5 business days on your assets, access or approvals moves the delivery date by the length of the wait. The price does not move.
Scope freeze
Scope freezes when month one's calendar is approved. Everything after that is a quote, approved in writing before work starts.
Minimum term
Three months, then month to month, thirty days notice. Six months makes the diagnostic free. Twelve months takes 12 percent off the monthly and locks your price.
Payment
Invoiced monthly in advance, bank transfer or card, in US dollars.
Never included
Ad spend, video shoots, actors, locations and paid media. If you need them we name someone good and take nothing for it.
Who does the work
A named person in Manila who signs your deliverables and is on your calls. Not a rotating pool, and not a ticket queue with a first name attached.

The rest, including ownership, confidentiality and governing law, is on the terms page.

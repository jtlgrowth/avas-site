01 / 08

What you actually build

Five AI employees. Yours.

Running before you leave the room.

01

The one that finds leads

Goes looking for people already saying they need what you sell, and hands them to you every morning while you are still asleep.

02

The one that answers DMs

Replies in your voice, qualifies the person, and books the call. You only step in when it is time to close.

03

The one that writes

A month of content that sounds like you and not like a robot. Captions, carousels, emails, all of it.

04

The one that does the admin

Inbox, follow ups, bookings, the reminders you keep forgetting. The invisible work that eats your evenings.

05

The one that remembers

Your second brain. Every SOP, client detail and decision, so the others get smarter instead of starting fresh.

+

And how to actually delegate to them

Most people build one and abandon it a week later. You leave with the rules for what to hand over, what to check, and when to trust it.
